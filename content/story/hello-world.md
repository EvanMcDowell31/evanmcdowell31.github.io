Pull up a chair. This site is my front porch on the internet: a place to share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the small tools and links I keep coming back to.

## What's here

- **Story** is where the longer writing lives: notes on engineering, working with AI agents, and the occasional detour.
- **Library** is my shelf: documents worth keeping, bookmarks I trust, and scripts I reach for.
- **Search** finds anything across all of it. On a keyboard, press `/` from any page.

## How it's built

It's a [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) site, written in Go templates and deployed to GitHub Pages on every push. No trackers and no framework. Just HTML, a little CSS and a pinch of JavaScript for search.

Thanks for stopping by.
